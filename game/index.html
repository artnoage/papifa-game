Pray (2 per match)
Jesus is so strong he uses both prayers; Mary and St George use one each. Hold R for 2 s to fill the cross, then press 1 for Jesus (unstoppable next shot, 15 s), 2 for Mother Mary (glowing goal shield, 15 s), or 3 for St George (white horse, +30% speed, 15 s).Push and hold the right stick: left for Jesus, up for St George, right for Mother Mary. Keyboard only

Space passes or tackles (Shift + Space slides). Hold F to shoot · tap F far from goal to knock it ahead. E plays a through ball. Q plays a one-two with the ball, or switches (tap) / calls a press (hold) without it. C chips. Double-tap Shift for a knock-on. Hold R for 2 s to pray, then press 1 (Jesus), 2 (Mary), or 3 (George). P or Esc pauses · M mutes.
